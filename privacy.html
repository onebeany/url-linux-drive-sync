URL Linux Drive Sync

Privacy notice

The utility accesses Google Drive only after the owner authorizes it. It uses file content and metadata to display files, copy files, detect changes, and synchronize the owner’s selected folders. Files are stored on the owner’s computer and Google Drive according to the configured synchronization rules.

OAuth credentials are kept on the owner’s Linux computer. The utility has no developer-operated data collection server and adds no advertising, analytics, or sale of user data. It does not use Google Drive data to train AI models.

The owner can stop the local services and revoke the app’s access through their Google Account at any time. Revoking access does not automatically delete existing local or Drive copies. Existing copies remain subject to the owner’s storage and deletion choices and Google Drive’s normal policies.

This page describes the utility itself; other programs the owner chooses to give local file access to are outside its scope.
